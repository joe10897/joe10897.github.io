I. Introduction & Motivation

The Motorcycle Safety Crisis

Paper ID: 20

In high-density mixed traffic, motorcycle riders are the Vulnerable Road Users (VRUs). Traffic conflicts and blind spots directly amplify injury and fatality risks.

  • Asymmetrical Protection: Motorcycles lack rigid protective structural frames, causing a fatality rate up to 28 times higher than passenger cars.
  • Environmental Blind Spots: Riders must react in split-seconds to improper lane changes, tailgating, or sudden braking.
  • No Dedicated Solutions: Commercial ADAS and radars are designed for cars — bulky, expensive, and impossible to install on budget 125cc scooters.
61.74%

of all fatal & injury traffic accidents in Taiwan (2023) involve motorcycles — the highest proportion among all vehicle types.

1 in 3

victims are young riders aged 18 to 24 — lack of experience, tailgating, and failure to notice road conditions are the leading causes.

Cars now have ADAS / Radar / AEB / BSD. Motorcycles have nothing.

The V.I.S.O.R. Proposal

Paper ID: 20

We propose the visually intelligent, cost-effective V.I.S.O.R. system

A low-cost, pure-vision, all-around active safety shield built for the motorcycle community — turning a passive helmet into an active electronic protection system.

Key Design Pillars
  • Dual-Direction Monitoring: Front 120° + rear 160° lenses → ~280° blind-spot-free coverage.
  • Dynamic Threat Zoning: Instantly classifies threat entry points, auto-switching front/rear alarms based on approach vector.
  • 3D Spatialized Audio: Helmet speakers pinpoint threat direction — left-rear, right-rear, front — enabling intuitive reflexive response without looking.

II. Literature Review & Foundation

Edge AI Computing & YOLO Selection

Paper ID: 20

Edge Computing Environment

Raspberry Pi 5

Broadcom BCM2712, 4× Cortex-A76 @ 2.4GHz, 8GB RAM

  • CPU-only — No GPU / CUDA dependency
  • <$100 — Affordable for mass adoption
  • Constraint: ~4× slower than desktop x86 CPU

Why YOLOv8n?

In this paper, we adopt YOLOv8n (Nano) — a proven, stable anchor-free detector with 3.2M parameters.

  • Anchor-Free Detection: Eliminates anchor box hyperparameter tuning — adapts naturally to varied motorcycle viewpoints.
  • Lightweight & Proven: Only 3.2M parameters — fits in Pi 5 memory with room for lane detection and tracking. Extensive community validation ensures stability.
  • Optimized for 320px: Downscaled input reduces compute matrix by 75% while preserving detection accuracy after domain-adaptation fine-tuning.
Future work: We plan to upgrade to YOLOv26n (2.6M params, superior mAP) — I'll discuss this further in the Future Roadmap section.

Key insight: Classical filters (Canny, Sobel) fail in tunnels, rain, or at night. An end-to-end CNN + self-attention architecture infers contours even when objects are partially occluded — no manual threshold recalibration needed.

Distributed Edge Compute Architecture

Paper ID: 20

Distributed Architecture: Decoupling Compute-Intensive AI from Real-Time Display

Why Dual-Node Architecture?

Running AI inference, frame acquisition, and display rendering on a single SBC often leads to latency and stuttering due to resource contention.

  • Raspberry Pi 5 (Core Engine): Dedicated to 320px video stream acquisition, deep learning inference, and object tracking.
  • Raspberry Pi Zero 2W (HUD Node): Small form factor, ultra-low power. Installed in the helmet/dashboard, it receives lightweight telemetry and drives the OLED screen.

III. Methodology & Novel Contributions

The Mathematics of Visual Safety

Paper ID: 20

1. Monocular Distance & Relative Motion

V.I.S.O.R. utilizes geometric similar triangle projection and a Kalman-like Exponential Moving Average (EMA) filter for distance and speed estimation:

$$d^{(t)} = \max\left(0, \frac{W_{real} \times f}{w_{bbox}^{(t)}} - d_{offset}\right)$$
$$v_{rel}^{(t)} = \alpha \cdot \frac{d^{(t-1)} - d^{(t)}}{\Delta t} + (1 - \alpha) \cdot v_{rel}^{(t-1)}$$

$W_{real}$ = physical width, $f = 800\text{px}$, $d_{offset} = 3.0\text{m}$, $\alpha = 0.4$

2. Time-To-Collision (TTC) & 3-Tier Threat

Rider warning triggers combine physical time-to-collision and relative image expansion rate:

$$TTC^{(t)} = \frac{d^{(t)}}{\max\left(v_{rel}^{(t)}, 0.1\right)}$$
$$\text{Area\_Ratio} = \frac{A^{(t)}}{A^{(t-8)}} > 1.25 \quad \cap \quad \Delta y_{centroid} > 0$$
  • Looming Alarm: bounding box area expands >25% over 8 frames + centroid moves downward.

3-Tier Threat Classification

⛔ CRITICAL < 1.0s< 2.0mIN_LANE

⚡ CAUTION < 2.5s< 8.0mAll

Edge filter: x<10% or >90% → safe. New objects (age<3) capped at CAUTION.

III. Methodology & Novel Contributions

Virtual Lane Filtering & ID Tracking

Paper ID: 20

Centroid Tracking & Coordinate Smoothing

To prevent flickering bounding boxes caused by vibrations or lighting changes, we implement a CentroidTracker with:

  • Euclidean Distance Association: Minimizes quadratic Euclidean distance of centroids across frames to maintain unique IDs.
  • Cache Window (Max Disappeared): Retains object tracks for up to 5 consecutive frames if occluded or missed by the detector.
  • Kalman Filter Integration: Smooths out bounding box coordinates over time to reduce jitter.

LSTR Lane Curve Model

$$x = \frac{c_0}{(y-c_1)^2} + \frac{c_2}{(y-c_1)} + c_3 + c_4 y - c_5$$
  • • EMA 平滑: α=0.4 / 突變降為 0.08 + Coasting 15 幀
  • • Logspace Y-Sampling: 50 點（近密遠疏）
  • • Skip-2 跳幀: 每 3 幀推論，攤銷 ~17ms/幀

Virtual Lane Filtering

In dense urban streets, parked vehicles along curbs often cause severe false alarms (False Positives).

  • Dynamic Virtual Lane: Filters out distractions by locking onto the active lane region (X-axis ratio 0.25 to 0.75).
  • Threat Escalation: Collision risk is evaluated only when the predicted trajectory of a tracked vehicle intersects the virtual lane.

Hardware-Software Ecosystem

Paper ID: 20

Distributed Processing Pipeline

To eliminate frame delay, V.I.S.O.R. decouples heavy perception tasks from real-time display rendering:

1. Perception Layer (Dual CSI Input)
Front Camera (IMX219, 120° FOV) & Rear Camera (OV5647, 160° FOV) feed MJPEG frames directly.
↓
2. Core Computing Unit (Raspberry Pi 5)
Process 320px frames on CPU-only. Run YOLOv8n (3.2M) and LSTR CULane in TorchScript.
↓
3. HUD Node (Raspberry Pi Zero 2W)
Receives coordinates and alarms via UDP broadcast (2.4GHz AP, <15ms latency).
↓
4. Output Terminal (SSD1315 HUD & Audio)
OLED (128x64) renders visual cues with hard full-screen inversion to instantly alert riders.

IV. Results & Performance Validation

VISOR Mobile App Interface

Paper ID: 20

Cross-Platform Tactical Console

We developed a cross-platform mobile app (Cordova — Android & iOS) that serves as the rider's command center. It displays a real-time dashboard with speed, lean angle, and directional threat levels — all synced from the Pi 5 core over BLE and Wi-Fi.

E-SOS Emergency Rescue

If the system detects an abnormal motorcycle tilt exceeding 15 seconds, it automatically transmits an emergency SMS containing the rider's GPS coordinates to pre-set emergency contacts. No rider action needed — fully autonomous crash response.

Gemini 2.5 Flash AI Analysis

Integrated with Google Gemini, the system analyzes historical riding data — speeding events, hard braking, dangerous lean angles — and auto-generates personalized AI safety recommendations tailored to each rider's behavior patterns.

  • BLE Auto-Pairing: visor_bt_agent.py — pair once, auto-connect every ride.
  • Supabase Cloud Sync: Telemetry permanently stored, with real-time TerminalConsole log streaming.
